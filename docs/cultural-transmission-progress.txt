VERIFIED BOUNDED CULTURAL TRANSMISSION AND SUPPORTED GRAMMAR FIRST USE
2026-11-11

This completes the explicitly bounded cultural/innovation evaluation in the
remaining-language-goal.txt contract. It does not prove unrestricted invention
of grammatical or semantic categories, biological artificial life, natural

Predeclared staged-cohort experiment

CultureTrialSweep runs 12 fixed seeds (0 through 11), five conditions, three
staged generations per condition, five learners per cohort and eight public-event
opportunities per learner. All 180 cohorts / 900 learner episodes and failures are
retained. These are nested observations, not 900 independent populations.

Lexicon.child supplies the existing weak inheritance mechanism. I, IS, HURT and
WALK lexical priors are explicitly normalized to the teacher's words to isolate
syntax from lexical acquisition and birth mishearing. Every learner starts with
zero adult productive construction mastery. No parent private history, adult
receptive profiles or word-formation mastery is copied. The previous cohort's
first learner becomes the next teacher; there is no selection for success.

The tested surface change omits IS, using subject/object order and an implicit
copula. This is an already supported engine construction, not a newly invented
role or semantic category. The seeded-contact founder begins with a declared
strength-nine compact convention. The supported-first-use founder begins with
ZERO constructions, deliberately exercises the engine's authored omission option,
then rehearses its actually emitted form. This distinguishes a seeded convention
from first use and learning by an actor. The intervention is explicit; spontaneous
invention is not claimed.

Disconnected learners receive no examples. Frozen learners receive no learning
updates. Full-contact learners hear the ordinary full construction. Adaptive
contact learns only from actual raw sounds and a synthetic independently public
injury event. WALK is never a lesson event. Every cohort is evaluated against
fixed full and compact WALK sentences, using its own production and decoding.
These fixed benchmarks avoid confusing a control's return to full speech with
successful retention of a compact founder convention.

Results, across 180 learner episodes in each condition:
  seeded-contact:       180 adopt compact; 180 understand held-out compact
  supported-first-use:  180 adopt compact; 180 understand held-out compact
  disconnected:           0 adopt compact;   0 understand held-out compact
  frozen:                 0 adopt compact;   0 understand held-out compact
  full-contact:           0 adopt compact;   0 understand held-out compact

All 900 learners still understand the fixed full WALK sentence. No learner starts
with adult productive mastery, and the sweep records no failed acceptance checks.
Compact speech persists through three adaptive staged generations while the full
community retains full production; the controlled communities diverge in surface
convention without losing their shared full-sentence comprehension. These results
apply to the explicit lexical priors, exposure schedule and allowed pattern.
They do not establish natural rates of cultural change, an optimal grammar,
selection pressure, long-term demographic stability or population scaling.

Native contact-chain experiment

runs/language-culture-v2 uses the exact pinned language JAR and compiled classes
already verified for teaching and native voices. It copies only retained build
files, libraries and archived sources into a NEW runtime/world. It never opens,
advances or rewrites the baseline world. Concurrent unrelated working edits are
preserved and excluded from the pinned experiment.

Four original stationary NoAI villagers occupy a stone platform. The source adult
starts with full plains SVO and ZERO constructions. Three sparse learner/control
lexicons use child inheritance plus the declared equal word priors. The source
and bridge each receive one real setup native injury. A physical barrier blocks
the endpoint from seeing the source, while the bridge can see both; a separate
wall isolates the comparison. Hearing a fact for learning requires joint visible
grounding under the existing speech rules. This is a visual encounter topology,
not a measured sound-occlusion model.

Controlled 200-tick opportunities use Speech.innovateObservedState for an actual
visible injury. The bridge sends a relay only after its own grammar selects the
learned compact form. Its emitted words come from its own vocabulary/production;
no source intention, hidden culture ID or source grammar is passed to the endpoint.
The bridge's own injury grounds the relay fact. The endpoint learns from those
actual utterances, then generates and understands the never-taught compact WALK
combination. The isolated control knows the same words but cannot infer the
missing syntax without contact.

The final native run passes at tick 840 after five controlled source opportunities
and three actual bridge relays. All four original bodies survive. Exact saved
language, voice memory, beliefs, lessons and commitments reopen before restart
decisions. The held-out construction remains usable after restart; a frozen
hearing phase preserves saved learned culture. First/restart/frozen all pass.
No post-setup body, vocabulary, grammar or inventory setters are used. There is no
new food or physical capability, and no claim of actual births or natural maturation.

The experiment demonstrates controlled multi-hop spread of a supported first use,
retention, generalization and a valid unexposed comparison. It does not establish
that the engine invented a new grammatical category. The symbolic staged cohorts
and native relay serve different purposes; they are not presented as natural
biological generations.

Further research

Natural contact schedules and family development under survival pressure,
population-scale compute/audio cost, longer-term changing environments, meaningful
selection pressures, richer semantics/pragmatics and actual new category formation
remain research work. Native audio quality also needs client listening assessment.
The completed bounded engineering contract must not be described as having solved
these problems or created unrestricted artificial life.
