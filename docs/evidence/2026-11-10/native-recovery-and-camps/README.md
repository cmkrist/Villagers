# Native recovery and camp work

The primary native world stopped normally at game time 60017 after a 6000-tick
recovery observation. Sage's saved native body rose from 1 health to 19.8333;
Alder finished at 20. Food came from actual owned meals and donations. All 179
existing humanoid UUIDs remained present. The 33 managed villagers include ten
newly enrolled existing residents, not ten births. The older authored save's
pinned files remained unchanged.

Two independent copied recovery observations passed before this primary run,
including a repeat at normal 20 TPS. Earlier copied failures remain in `runs/`:
Sage died from falls in those copies. No failed copy was promoted over the main
world, and no body was resurrected or supplied with health or items.

Native camp tests verified existing-body enrollment without supplies, real hay
harvesting, bread recipes, sharing, and native storage withdrawals. Six earlier
founders survived an 8000-tick copy at full health after roof-directed movement
was corrected. Later tests use one existing local pair, preserving saved founders
when continued. These observations have not yet proved new housing or births. Tests before v14
did not guarantee continuous founder residency: v13 exposed an unforced western
corner and one founder stopped ticking. Their health snapshots are not continuous
endurance proof. v14 fixed this, with complete camp chunk coverage and per-founder
age-progress guards; it stopped after 20,000 simulated ticks when a captive
villager reached 4 health. Primary remained unchanged at 60017.

The separate native bed recipe audit uses only an in-memory recipe fixture. It
confirms one log and twelve strings become one bed and one spare plank. It grants
no supplies to actors and is not evidence of camp production. Actual material
collection, safe bed placement, paid breeding, renewable food, and the villager's
dragon defeat still require gameplay evidence. The game-completion goal remains
active.
