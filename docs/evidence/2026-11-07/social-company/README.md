# Autonomous social visit acceptance

The final unreleased 0.4.1-dev build passed a bounded native social visit and a
save/reopen check. A lonely Dara chose the farther liked Ember, walked to her,
and completed forty ticks of willing contact. Dara remembered visiting Ember;
Ember remembered welcoming Dara. Dara's loneliness finished at0.65782 from0.85,
both participants gained0.004expressed sociability experience, and their actual
conversation increased mutual regard. Iris continued her own ordinary behavior.
All three original bodies survived.

This fixture declares a peaceful enclosed platform, three adults with six bread
each, initial loneliness and three prior food-favour opinions for Dara toward
Ember. After initial setup, the ordinary controller makes every choice. The probe
uses no later actor, need, clock, position, decision or terrain setters. It
observes natural movement rather than calling the social routine directly.

On reopening, ENTITY_LOAD checks verified all original managed bodies, bread,
exact saved opinions, visit memories and experience before autonomous decisions
could change them. The reopened server then closed normally. Both native phases
exited0; their exact PID/start identities are terminal.

The final source archive matches the current production/test/probe Java files.
Artifact hashes and process receipts are under `combined/`; `results.json` summarizes
the acceptance and `build.log` records345Java plus10Python passing checks. The
initial accepted visit, before the activity-label/expressed-personality and
bounded sight-scan refinements, is retained separately under `initial/`. The
pre-forestry-change acceptance is retained under `final/`; the combined run
includes the subsequent concurrent forestry guards without changing their source.

`runner.py` is the retained test runner; the maintained version is

server and its owner-accepted EULA. Reproduce only with a fresh runtime, for
example `--runtime-name social-company-recheck`, and the JDK25 environment used
by `tools/test.sh`.

This establishes an autonomous friend visit in a controlled scene and retention
of its consequences. Larger social groups, obstacle-heavy travel, long-term
variety and hostile-village survival remain open. Trip/cooldown state is transient
