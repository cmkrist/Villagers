# Paid preparation, charitable delivery and resumable market approaches

Final frozen source passed **376 Java tests and 10 Python checks**. Three native
fixtures and all three clean restarts passed against that build. The detached
main classes matched each loaded mod JAR, with no production-source drift at
collection. [results.json](results.json) binds source snapshots, artifacts,
receipts, exact process custody and terminal outcomes. Raw observations and probe
sources are retained separately for accepted, prior-source and failed runs.

A paid proposal previously reused the charitable food-production signal. A
native boundary check reproduced the resulting unauthorized gift on the prior
build: a generous producer heard an affordable paid FOOD request, then the
existing charitable delivery method transferred food when explicitly invoked by
the probe. That invocation was an intentional denial check, not an autonomous
successful market exchange. Its failure, resulting world and original artifact
remain in `retained-failure/food-commission-prior-source/`. The identical probe
source is bound to prior and final paid-request runs.

FoodDemand now records GIFT or TRADE alongside its one known requester. Only
GIFT authorizes charitable travel, immediate delivery and meal deferral. A paid
request still raises the reserve15→20 and uses ordinary production, but waits
for the actual negotiated purchase. Gift completion/withdrawal cannot silently
consume the paid signal; a sale completes TRADE. A later actual request from the
same peer can change intent. Timestamp replay, another peer, expiry, rewind and
unload guards remain. The two intents do not add two production queues or create
an order, debt, reserved money, material authority or off-screen transaction.

The initial typed-source paid-request and charitable-delivery tests passed, but

food4 and failed the same gate. Both adults retained full observed health. Both failed

exposed blocked buyer routes and the need for prepared goods to trigger delivery.
No failed world was restored, resupplied or substituted into an accepted history.

A funded hungry buyer now gives its bounded food-market attempt priority over
another provisioning trip or charitable request. Immediate owned meals, survival
arbitration, social reactions and pending charitable delivery keep their prior
precedence. The market still yields on cooldown, refusal, body restrictions,
failed or exhausted routes and bounded journey deadlines. Partner discovery does
not read peer inventory or private needs. Contact requires real mutual sight,
hearing and reach, and every payment still checks actual goods, coins, capacity,
consent and reserves.

at most three native paths per admitted batch, rechecks each stand's current
eligibility, and does not discard later candidates after three rejected paths.

admission, loaded/home paths, travel/stall clocks and queue bounds remain.
Unit scenarios cover a reachable fourth candidate, changed eligibility,
interruption and all343 rejected candidates within115 batches. These are policy
checks, not proof that all343 stands are natively reachable. Path diagnostics now
retain actual rejected as well as accepted query results and both eye positions.

to its actual heard paid requester. It uses only the known identity and a visible

reserve. Its600tick offer budget bounds fresh incoming quotes, which can answer

productive fixture exercised one actual producer-initiated paid delivery.
