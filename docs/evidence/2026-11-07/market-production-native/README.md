## Scope and ownership

This is one finite cycle in a supplied peaceful two-room fixture: beds, tables,
water, lit ceiling, physical counter and twelve initially mature wheat crops;
selected greedy/glutton maker and greedy/frugal farmer with an initially common
language. The maker starts one log, a used working hoe and three bread. The farmer
starts six carrots, sixteen seeds and two marked coins through real finite player
gifts, with no tool or bread. It runs80TPS with400base exhaustion/day. There are no
post-setup supplies, fixture body/clock/growth/position setters or scripted
manufacture, trade, harvest or meal.

This establishes customer-triggered paid production and returned currency, not
indefinite income, natural tree/crop regrowth, normal-speed survival, calibrated
prices, population capacity or a complete local economy. The maker ended at5food
after eating; food security remains fragile. Death/crash accounting, repeated
productive cycles, durable proposal budgets, native foreign/denied quotes,
inequality and larger/hostile villages remain open. Small-scene tick snapshots
are not capacity evidence.
