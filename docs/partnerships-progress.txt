Relationship behavior

Friendship remains each villager's own opinion and actual shared experiences.
It is distinct from a partnership, and a partnership is distinct from parenthood.
An actor can propose a partnership after sufficient personal contact, affection
and trust. Its own health, food, fatigue, fear, mood, temperament, commitments and
separation cooldown affect willingness. The recipient evaluates its own state
independently. Friendship does not guarantee reciprocal attraction or acceptance;
refusing a partnership does not incur an automatic social penalty.

A structural close-kin safeguard can block a proposal. It does not give either
actor the other's private ancestry or feelings. The previous runtime shortcut
that attached unspoken parent UUIDs to family offers has been removed. Existing
ancestry and family-history records remain intact.

The proposer speaks a relationship request, the receiver must parse it, the
receiver speaks its own partner promise, the proposer must parse that promise,
and the proposer speaks a reciprocal promise which the receiver must parse.
Generic YES, WANT FRIEND, a child request, partial understanding or a past-tense
statement cannot form a partnership. Speech is sounds and punctuation only;
there is no LLM call or intended-clause input to an interpreter. The utterance
receiver is the actual direct conversational participant, not an inferred hidden
third party. The sentence translation surface recognizes the listener's parsed
pragmatic frames, e.g. 'Will you be my partner?'.

One active explicit partnership is supported per person. This is an authored
exclusive-partnership model, not a claim that all real societies work this way.
Each mind retains its own saved receipt, peer identity, agreement time, ended
history and actual shared-visit history. Both saved receipts must match before
mutual family permission exists. A partially saved pair cannot grant consent,
copy the missing receipt or silently force the other person's commitment.
An inconsistent pair can reaffirm its commitments only through fresh reciprocal
spoken promises. The earlier account and its shared visits remain in ended
RECONFIRMED history; it is not silently copied into the other mind. An existing
commitment to a different person still blocks acceptance.

Commitments affect ordinary company target preference using the actor's own
saved relationship. They do not reveal a partner's unseen location, override
home/navigation permissions, bypass attention cooldowns, force a visit, or prevent
friendship with other villagers. Actual completed willing company updates opinion
and persistent shared history; travel, a spoken promise and repeated nearby ticks
do not earn shared visits. Shared-visit credit has a 400-tick minimum interval.
A newly made vow receives no retroactive shared-visit completion.

Withdrawal and absence

Either participant can withdraw its own consent immediately. Negative personal
regard or fear can cause the controller to do this even while the partner is
absent. This changes only the actor's own relationship account and invalidates
mutual family permission. Unloading someone does not reveal their current state
or location. A nearby partner must independently decode the withdrawal before its
own record is updated. An unintelligible or unheard withdrawal cannot force the
actor to stay; it can leave the other's belief unresolved. A later actual contact
can repeat the withdrawal, respecting a finite relationship attention budget.
Withdrawal does not delete prior shared history, ancestry or children.

At most eight partnership histories are retained; the active record is protected
while old ended histories can be forgotten. A 2400-tick reconsideration interval
prevents immediate automatic recommitment after a separation. A new proposal has
a 1200-tick initiation cooldown. Both speakers have a separate 200-tick relationship
attention budget, so relationship speech cannot starve urgent food requests.
These counters/budgets are session state; the commitments themselves are saved.

Separate parenthood consent and resources

A family invitation now explicitly asks about a CHILD rather than describing
friendship and a bed. A child promise is a different decoded act from a partner
promise. New family invitations and family-company approaches select an existing
partner. Each parent independently checks its current willingness and owned
family-food reserve. Existing child consent remains a short-lived agreement;
a saved partnership does not resurrect it after restart.

Birth and King Size Bed visits require BOTH matching persistent partner receipts
and matching current child agreements. Existing age, health, safety, kinship,
food costs, planting reserve, nursery, occupancy, population, route and capability
checks remain authoritative. A future-dated commitment cannot authorize a present
family action. Partnership does not create a bed, food, child or replacement body;
withdrawal invalidates family permission even before the peer understands it.

Persistence and compatibility

Mind schema 14 adds partnership history. Older saves start with no invented
partnership while preserving names, words, cultural origins, opinions, learned
policy, episodes, ancestry and identities. Existing parents are not retroactively
assigned spouses or erased. Children do not inherit their parents' commitments.
Unknown future schemas retain existing protection. Frozen checkpoints can use
saved commitments but do not learn new partnerships, shared histories, withdrawals
or family invitations.
