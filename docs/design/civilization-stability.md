# Civilization stability acceptance

The target is an ordinary persistent village that renews its food and tools,
distributes meals, rests safely, raises children and continues after save/reload.
A successful supplied fixture is one prerequisite, not this entire target.

Acceptance must preserve actual deaths and resource costs. No post-setup food,
wood, coins, bodies, health, crop growth, weather or time may be supplied or reset.
Source, loaded artifact, setup, duration and restart evidence must be identified.

The immediate gates are:

1. Four-adult division of labour: real input purchases, two independently paid
   tool orders, native farming, income-funded food and no starvation damage.
   Repeat with only three initial maker bread to exercise reserve/work priority.
2. Renewable food endurance: native planting, crop growth, repeat harvest,
   bread production, actual sharing and consumption over multiple loaded days.
   Preserve the same bodies and verify inventory/nutrition at entity load.
3. Renewable materials: natural tree regrowth and a second authorized harvest,
   repeated tool replacement and renewed grants without ownership guesses.
4. Natural terrain: every resident can reach food and shelter, with actual paid
   construction where required. A partial native path is never success.
5. Population: ordinary births and child survival without outgrowing actual food,
   beds or safe working space; no replacement residents counted as survivors.
6. Performance: whole-mod tick cost, path queue, IO backlog and tail server tick
   times under a declared population. Small-fixture means establish no capacity.

## Current scheduling boundary

Immediate eating and survival precede production. A real understood tool order
may seek its wood input and perform native recipes before replenishing the
optional pantry target only while the existing combined body/owned-food admission
threshold still holds. It retains the worker’s actual working tool and rechecks
food, safety, station, recipe and output capacity at commit.

An unstocked food respondent must possess enough wheat for a recipe, or a working
hoe and planting material, before accepting preparation. This is an apparent
route, not a guarantee of crops, reachability, station access or future delivery.
Actual understood local words and successful purchases create or refresh a
short-lived supplier memory. Expiry, unload and failed food travel/contact discard
it. Successful input purchases retain the actual supplier for the next heard
order; they create no new order or stock guarantee. No remote stock is queried.

Paid food preparation and charity remain distinct intents; one does not authorize
the other. Refusal diagnostics report commodity and reason without extra speech,
background tasks or unbounded history.

Charitable food requests rotate over at most sixteen visible adult neighbours,
remembering tried identities rather than a distance-order cursor. A refused or
empty nearest neighbour cannot monopolize subsequent attempts. Existing hearing,
request cooldown, willingness, home bounds and complete-path checks still apply;
only physical discovery order changes.

The four-founder endurance fixture supplies two generous/frugal farmers with
iron hoes and seeds, two glutton dependents, marked starting bread, forty-eight
age-zero mixed crops, water, light, two tables and six beds. Normal metabolism,
sharing, social behaviour and breeding run at eighty TPS. It requires at least
two loaded days, repeated native regrowth/harvest, consumed produced food for both
dependents, no zero-food interval or damage, and exact saved inventory/nutrition
checks at entity load. Any ordinary descendants join the survival census. This
prepared peaceful setting does not establish hostile natural-village survival,
indefinite tool renewal, autonomous house building or population capacity.

Rejected family navigation can clear the current elective action during execution.
The controller now checks that an action still exists before timeout/completion
credit and renders a valid idle activity after rejection. A missing action is an
ordinary transition, not a reason to crash the server or reward failed travel.
The dedicated regression checks the actual FamilyCompany rejection transition.
