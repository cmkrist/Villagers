# Four Biomes War

## Separate native-generation map

The user chose a separate world with the four regions retained and native terrain
and structures. Launch `./tools/play-native-regions.sh` to open
`runs/native-four-regions-play/saves/NativeFourRegions`. The original
`runs/four-biomes-play/saves/FourBiomesWar` remains a separate preserved save.

Preset `villagers:native_four_regions` uses vanilla `minecraft:noise` with
`minecraft:overworld` settings and the existing four-quarter biome source:
northwest plains, northeast forest, southwest frozen peaks, southeast desert.
Terrain, carving, vegetation, ores, villages and other structures use native
Minecraft generation. No authored settlement construction or supplied residents
run in this mode. Nether and End use their vanilla generators. Native village
biome eligibility remains unchanged; forests/frozen peaks need not have villages.

The new map retains the1024block border and seed846206. It locates a native
village with96blocks of border clearance and starts the observer nearby; native
buildings and residents are never moved. For this seed, the starting settlement
is a native desert village atX352/Z160. The natural-village observer/adoption
workflow runs in this separate development world and retains its own UUID cohort.

Native generation and restart tests in `runs/native-regions-world-v3` found eight
natural villagers,95native desert-village jigsaw pieces and correct actual biome
palettes in all four corners. An independent full-pack client created and reopened
the playable save. This establishes generation/persistence, not sustained NPC
survival or the broader game-completion goal.

User-requested scenario, implemented2026-11-09. World creation, expanded creature
minds, autonomous faction combat, save/reopen and native trader interaction have
acceptance evidence. See [battle evidence](../evidence/2026-11-09/four-biomes-war/README.md).

The new `villagers:four_biomes_war` world preset uses a persistent custom biome
source and terrain generator. Its playable overworld is bounded at X/Z[-512,512),
64×64chunks. Each quarter is32×32chunks. The border limits the playable map;
Minecraft can generate padding chunks outside it for rendering and lighting.
Chunks outside the settlements generate when explored, rather than requiring
all4096chunks to remain loaded. No vanilla structures are generated by this preset.

| Quarter | Biome | Settlement | Faction | Center X/Z |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Northwest | Plains (Grassland) | Greenhaven | Villagers | -96/-96 |
| Northeast | Forest | Blackwood | Illagers | 96/-96 |
| Southwest | Frozen Peaks (Snowy Mountains) | Frostwatch | Illagers | -96/96 |
| Southeast | Desert | Sunwell | Villagers | 96/96 |

Terrain has rolling lowlands, dunes, raised snowy ridges and town terraces.
Native biome features supply vegetation. The generator currently has solid
stone/deepslate below the surface, without a natural cave network.

Each town starts with four houses, eight beds, two irrigated wheat fields,
workstations, eight residents, one wandering trader and one Enderman. Villager
towns have four sword-bearing residents and four farmers. Each illager town has
six crossbow pillagers and two axe-bearing vindicators. Initial infrastructure,
24bread per humanoid resident/trader, weapons, hoes and32seeds per farmer are explicit authored
scenario supplies. No casualty or resource restoration is implemented. These are
new founders; no population/history from any older trial is copied.

`four-biomes-war.json` records preparation before constructing the scenario and
records `active` after all four towns exist. An active save is never rebuilt.
An interrupted initial setup is stopped for inspection rather than silently
replaying construction or founders. The central24×24chunks are forced for town
and battlefield simulation. Existing trial worlds remain separate.

Run `./tools/play-war.sh` from the project to open the separate playable save at
`runs/four-biomes-play/saves/FourBiomesWar`. It stages verified pack assets into a
new client directory without copying any existing world or launcher account.
The launcher uses20TPS and has no automatic stop budget in ordinary use. Existing
war saves and casualties are preserved. The observed playable world has already
experienced combat; opening it continues that history. It resumes the saved
observer mode and position. Use `/gamemode survival` to join the world as a player.

With the mod installed elsewhere, select **Four Biomes War** as the world type to
start an independent campaign. The dev client also supports `VILLAGERS_TRIAL=war`
with the standard launcher. JDK25 is selected from explicit environment overrides,
PATH, or a locally installed companion toolchain.

## Creature minds and warfare

All scenario illagers, Endermen and wandering traders use `CreatureAgents` and the
same `AgentMind` personality, genome, drives, bounded action-value learner and
bounded memories as villagers. Creature snapshots live in the separate world
folder `creature-minds/<UUID>/mind.json`, keeping them out of villager ancestry
searches. They rest, explore, seek compatible companions and, for humanoids,
consume actual owned or discovered food. Endermen do not use human food metabolism;
they retain neutral self-defense and native teleport/physics behavior. Vanilla
goal writers are suppressed only for explicitly adopted creatures; navigation,
collision, item use and native merchant interactions remain active.

Outside this world, adoption is explicit with `/villagers creatures adopt`,
`/villagers creatures status` and `/villagers creatures release`, targeting the
nearest supported mob within24blocks. A released scenario creature stays released.
These commands support the illager superclass, including pillagers and vindicators,
Endermen and wandering traders. This is a new decision controller, not a recreation
of every vanilla spellcasting or profession routine.

Four residents in each town are expedition soldiers. Other residents keep local
survival/economy behavior and can respond to nearby enemies. After200saved world
ticks, soldiers march toward hostile towns through short complete native paths.
The authored campaign director knows settlement locations and loaded garrison
occupancy; individual combat targets require nearby enemies in sight. Empty enemy
towns are skipped. Wounded, hungry or exhausted combatants retreat and recover;
retreat tags persist in the native body save. Native crossbows charge and launch
projectiles; melee uses actual equipped weapons, native damage and durability.
Traders and Endermen are neutral. Crossfire can still cause friendly casualties.

The campaign director now orders an understrength town to rebuild its original
eight-adult population before further expeditions. Soldiers beyond32blocks of
home regroup there; once home, they can use ordinary food/rest/family routines.
Local combat and ordinary damage remain active. Juveniles, traders and Endermen
do not count toward the adult garrison, and no replacements or supplies appear.
`war status` includes `adultTownPopulations` for this authored strategic census.

`/villagers war status` reports the campaign. `war-campaign.json` saves observed
marches, successful enemy hits/damage, bounded recent events and permanent
casualties. The world clock and entity tags retain campaign progression across
reopen. No reinforcements or resurrection are provided; divine starvation
succession is disabled for this generator. Ordinary villager family guards remain.

## Verification so far

`WarMapTest` counts every chunk:1024per biome,4096total; checks one town per
biome, two towns per faction, flat town footprints and bounded terrain gradients.
Native `WarWorldServerProbe` inspects actual generated biomes, remote terrain,
16bed blocks per town, eight residents per town and40total founders. The separate
`WarBattleServerProbe` observes bilateral marches, native hits, an enemy-caused
death, learned attempts in all four added species, a paid native merchant
transaction and campaign/mind persistence. Both have restart modes. A separate
full-pack visible client created and reopened the playable world at20TPS; rendered
activity and continued native casualties were inspected.

Run the native check in a new isolated runtime using the existing prepared

The native probe is a test-only mod which stops the server after checking it.
Do not put that probe JAR in a playable client/server. Tests preserve their
worlds and logs under their runtime directory.

These checks establish this bounded scenario, not indefinite economic stability,
balanced factions, every vanilla mob ability, or unlimited population capacity.
Supplies are finite and deaths are real; a campaign can end with a defeated town
or faction. New independent campaigns use the world preset, without resetting the
observed world's history.

## Food production toward the game-completion goal

Managed scenario pillagers can now replenish a pantry from their town's existing
wheat. They inspect nearby visible ripe crops, harvest native loot, and reserve
one real seed from stock or that same loot before replanting age zero. Like a
player, they can harvest wheat by hand; this narrow wheat exception grants no
general block breaking, tilling, new planting sites or free tools. Protected crops
and soil are excluded. Other villager harvesting rules remain as documented.

Three real wheat can become bread only at an existing reachable town table,
using the loaded server recipe and detached inventory capacity checks. Public
town stations are authored knowledge, like town destinations. Native paths and
nearby wooden-door operation provide access; no actor is teleported. Searches,
path attempts, temporary blocked targets and work frequency are bounded.
Observed field locations enter the same bounded persistent farm-memory episodes
as villager observations. A provisioner with an empty pantry returns to a seen
field rather than drifting away between crop scans.

Pillagers expose a real bread in their offhand when they have pantry surplus and
can give one to a nearby same-town managed illager with an empty offhand. A
foodless illager can approach a visible bread holder. The transfer splits a real
stack. If no offer is visible and it has strayed beyond12blocks of its remembered
home, a foodless illager heads home to search locally. The recipient's ordinary
eating reflex consumes the actual meal. This uses visible
offers, not remote inspection of private inventories or minds. Vindicators can
receive meals; they do not acquire a new hidden inventory or farming capability.
Native pillager bags and equipment persist in the body save. The diagnostic
`foodWork` counters are per loaded session, not cumulative campaign totals.

The isolated `--food` native test uses two new empty illager founders,
existing ripe wheat and house tables,
and no later supplies or clock/growth/body setters. Other founders are explicitly
discarded only in this separate fixture to exclude warfare and outside meals.
Scenario founders and loose items are discarded during this fixture's setup.
An unspawned scratch body with one test seed verifies payment, immature-crop
rejection and native operator protection. A successful first phase requires
12harvests,4bread recipes, a real transfer and a produced-food meal in each body.
Restart checks native item counts/components, health, position and nutrition at
entity load before controller updates. Run with a fresh runtime, then reopen it:

```bash
python3 tools/war-world-server-test.py --server-home /path/to/prepared-server --runtime runs/new-food-check --food
python3 tools/war-world-server-test.py --server-home /path/to/prepared-server --runtime runs/new-food-check --food --restart
```

This gate tests a production route and persistence. It does not establish repeat
crop growth, sustained population growth, illager reproduction, or Nether/End
progression. The goal remains a verified villager defeat of the Ender Dragon.

The preset now includes the standard vanilla Nether and End. The earlier preset
contained only the Overworld. A stopped existing development save can add the
missing definitions with `tools/enable-adventure-dimensions.py --run-dir <runtime>`.
The tool locks the stopped client/world, preserves existing NBT values and seed,
adds only missing dimensions, backs up the original world-generation settings
and replaces that one file atomically. The actual primary save was upgraded and
its reopened server reports all three dimensions; see
[native dimension/population report](../evidence/2026-11-09/primary-growth/dimensions-and-population-report.json).
Portals, autonomous travel and End/dragon progression still require implementation.

The separate `--food-endurance` option implies `--food` and observes at least
24000ticks of both residents' actual loaded aging, stopping by144000world ticks.
Its pass additionally requires a second native
harvest of a previously replanted cell and at least two nutrition increases in
each initially empty founder, with no zero-food or damage interval. This uses
normal crop growth and exhaustion at80TPS. Per-session cell memory is capped at128;
its repeat counter can miss an evicted cell but cannot invent a repeated harvest.
Use a new runtime; a normal `--food --restart` can then check its saved state.

## Ordinary illager families under native validation

The new family controller is enabled by the existing breeding configuration.
Scenario residents reserve actual sheltered HOME POIs, preserving their ticket
and nursery tag through unload/reopen. Death, explicit release and permanent
discard release the reservation; ordinary chunk unload does not. Both nearby
parents must exchange and accept a local family invitation, have healthy bodies, adequate food,
non-low mood, acceptable fatigue and regard, no close kinship and no active
breeding cooldown. Visible local enemies block birth. Each parent must own six
bread and pays three, retaining a pantry reserve; offspring never carry weapons
or soldier tags. A separate free sheltered and natively reachable bed is required.

Offspring have two-parent inherited genes, language and lineage. Native illagers
have no vanilla ageable baby form; the controller marks a native scaled juvenile
body until24000loaded age ticks. Juveniles receive actual food offers and bypass
warfare and farming. Explicit release restores the ordinary native body scale.
The isolated two-producer fixture now has an ordinary birth, juvenile survival
with an actual owned-food meal, native maturation at24000loaded ticks and a
successful adult-child restart. Its records are in
[first birth](../evidence/2026-11-09/illager-first-birth.json),
[juvenile meal](../evidence/2026-11-09/illager-child-meal.json),
[maturation](../evidence/2026-11-09/illager-maturation.json), and
[adult reopen](../evidence/2026-11-09/illager-adult-reopen.json).
This establishes one isolated birth-to-adult cycle; repeat generations, sustained
population capacity and growth of the primary four-town campaign remain unproven.
Family offers use the existing bounded Courtship lifetime and hearer limit.
Ready adults approach a mutually agreed visible partner through a complete native
path. Consent expires and all readiness conditions are checked again at birth;
an invitation supplies no food or automatic success.

`--families` on the native runner uses two empty pillagers and enables breeding;
its initial gate stops at the first ordinary child and therefore proves no long
generation-survival result. `--server-port` permits independent loopback fixtures.
