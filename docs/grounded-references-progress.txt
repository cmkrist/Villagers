GROUNDED REFERENCE PROGRESS — FULL LANGUAGE GOAL REMAINS ACTIVE
2026-11-11

Scope

This implements the reference foundation within docs/remaining-language-goal.txt.
It does not finish source-aware reasoning, practical questions, persistent
partnerships/work agreements, receptive grammars, word formation or the cultural
transmission evaluation. Those remain required parts of the active goal.

Implemented behavior

Each villager and player glossary can retain up to 64 learned name-to-referent
bindings. Targets identify either a person or a particular place in a dimension.
Names are ordinary phonetic words; UUIDs and coordinates are never put on the
spoken wire. A speaker must know its own binding before using a reference.

Villagers announce their own spoken alias using a naming construction. Listeners
must understand that construction and actually see the speaker. Learning needs
three observations separated by at least 40 simulation ticks. Actual naming
opportunities have a separate 200-tick attention budget. A familiar sound with
multiple possible people/places remains ambiguous. A confidently incorrect
binding can produce a confidently incorrect interpretation.

Places are named only at an actual nearby loaded bed or crop block visible to
both participants. The speaker turns toward it and emits a public particle cue
to model pointing. The listener's binding comes from that joint-attention event,
not the intended sentence's metadata. The names can later be spoken without
transmitting coordinates. The server fixture verifies the observation adapter;
visual-client gesture UX has not been accepted.

Clause and Sentence preserve names in subject/object slots. Interpretation takes
only sounds and the listener's own vocabulary, grammar and reference memory.
The speaker's intended references remain diagnostics, never resolution inputs.
Confirmation preserves a named object instead of silently turning it into a
generic person. Generic PERSON/PLACE words cannot identify a private third party.

Conversations can describe a named third party who visibly holds real food, or
a previously named nearby place whose actual block supports the description.
The speaker's observation and the listener's testimony are recorded separately.
The latter uses identities/places reconstructed from the listener's bindings.
No reputation record or exact private episode is copied between minds.

Children inherit naming vocabulary, not their parents' private acquaintances or
maps. Existing sparse developing vocabularies remain sparse. Old saved names,
words and cultural origins are retained. Spoken aliases do not replace entity
UUIDs or Minecraft name tags. Individual naming/statement counters are diagnostic
session state; the reference memories and beliefs persist.

Compatibility

Mind schema 12 and glossary schema 4 add reference memory. Missing reference data
in older saves starts empty rather than inventing knowledge of other people.
Existing resolved-person belief representation is preserved. Unknown future
schemas remain protected. Word reinforcement, grammar learning and save/reload
preserve reference bindings. Lexical lessons repeat individual sounds, including
grammatical verbs, without routing those atoms through the sentence adapter.
