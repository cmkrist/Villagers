Implemented scope

This completes a bounded food-question/investigation slice of priority 2 in
remaining-language-goal.txt. It builds on the verified person/place references.
Persistent partnership/work agreements, receptive grammars, word formation,
childhood teaching and cultural evaluation remain required. This is not general
human reasoning, unrestricted language invention or completion of the full goal.

A healthy hungry villager without a usable meal can ask a visible neighbor who
has food. That neighbor parses the actual sounds in its own language and chooses
an answer from its own beliefs. An unknown answer means lack of knowledge,
not proof that no food exists. A complete answer must identify a person through
the asker's own name bindings or conversational I/YOU context. Confidently wrong
names remain wrong; the intended target is not given to the listener.

A later turn asks how the report is known. Both actors share the existing
200-tick dialogue attention budget. Replies distinguish a claimed sighting from
a claimed named source and describe the basis as recent or old. Names in source
attribution must also be understood. The original responder retains its own
answer context; it never passes that Entry to the asker's interpreter. Changed,
expired or displaced contexts cannot silently qualify an unrelated answer.

The asker's independently decoded account retains its immediate speaker, stated
root, evidence kind and conservative age. A reported sight remains HEARD, not
OBSERVED. Unqualified and unknown sources get lower decision weights. Old or
attribution that resolves back to the asker cannot qualify a usable query result.
There is no omniscient social-network cycle detector: unspoken or mistranslated
origins remain uncertain. Repetition cannot
refresh the original age; multiple routes from the same stated root neither add
confidence nor reset that root's age. Ranking uses strongest evidence rather than
summing repeated reports. These are bounded heuristics, not calibrated truth
probabilities or proof that the reported witness told the truth.

Recent means within 400 simulation ticks. With a recent verbal age band, the
listener conservatively assumes the oldest possible basis in that band. Food
accounts expire within 600 ticks, rather than allowing fresh hearing to make old
food last indefinitely. This can reject useful fresh reports when the protocol
cannot distinguish a new sighting from repetition; it favors bounded uncertain
memory over invented precision. Exact observation timestamps are not spoken.

The decoded answer can redirect FoodHelp from the witness to the named provider
only if the seeker independently sees that provider within its existing search
radius and home permissions. Speech does not reveal an unseen current location.
Existing navigation, inventory, willingness, reach and recipient capacity checks
still govern the request and transfer. Receiving a real item records a separate
observed past GIVE FOOD event. It does not assert the donor still owns more food.

An understood report of no food reduces a lead but remains testimony. Actual
observation can contradict prior testimony while retaining its attribution.
A failed transfer is not proof of absent food or deliberate refusal. Stock
reports distinguish physical possession from shareable meals: a reserved final
planting root still exists. Only the owner examines its own bag; third-party
food observations use public held food, visibility and learned names.

Urgent hunger/injury uses the existing direct request path. Healthy inquiries
have a finite 600-tick context and a 1200-tick initiation cooldown; failed or
unknown answers fall back to ordinary requests. Pending source questions prevent
incidental healthy food requests from consuming the asker's food-request budget.
Frozen mode does not initiate these conversations or mutate this knowledge.

Supported surface vs autonomous behavior

WHO FOOD, WHERE FOOD and SOURCE frames have independent production/interpretation
checks. Autonomous FoodHelp currently uses WHO FOOD plus SOURCE. Named place
answers are supported and unit-tested, but an autonomous crop/site investigation
policy is not claimed. Source chains, circularity, stale accounts, conflicting
reports and confidently wrong identities have automated checks. The native
fixture demonstrates a direct witness; multi-hop social diffusion remains part
of the later cultural evaluation.

Persistence

Mind schema 13 adds provenance to saved beliefs. Schema-12 accounts migrate as
unqualified testimony from the already-known immediate speaker; no hidden
original witness is invented. Existing observation remains observation. Existing
words, references, cultural origin and identities are preserved. The short-lived
question/answer attention context is session state, so restart cancels a pending
conversation while preserving the learned account. Future-schema protection
remains in place.
