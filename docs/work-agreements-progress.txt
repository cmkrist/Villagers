PERSISTENT WORK AGREEMENTS — FULL LANGUAGE GOAL REMAINS ACTIVE
2026-11-11

Scope and remaining priorities

This implements a bounded production-contract framework for priority 4 in
remaining-language-goal.txt. Supported concrete jobs are making one wooden hoe
from wood, and making one bread from wheat, followed by paid delivery. This is
not a general profession/career, labor-law, wage, credit or limitless task system.
The full goal remains ACTIVE: receptive grammar, word formation, childhood
teaching and controlled cultural/grammar-innovation evaluation remain required.

Spoken negotiation

An unfilled ordinary local food/tool request can now lead into explicit work
negotiation. Ready-stock trades retain their existing path. A potential customer
uses its own needs and possessed money; the worker evaluates its own readiness,
stock, materials, existing commitment, permitted recipe and known station.
The customer's needs are not discovered by reading a remote bag or private mind.

The request speaks the job output, material, quantity ONE, payment in emeralds,
a price of ONE or TWO, and the deadline convention SOON. SOON currently means
2400 simulation ticks after agreement, independent of wall-clock server speed.
The worker can counterquote its own price. The customer must independently decode
and accept that quote; the worker must decode the acceptance and speak an
acknowledgement; the customer must decode that acknowledgement. A generic YES,
partial phrase, unsupported material, quantity, deadline or foreign grammar cannot
stand in for understanding the terms. A confidently wrong price remains the
listener's wrong price; speaker intention never repairs it invisibly.

Interpretation takes sounds and the listener's own language. Each receipt is
constructed from that actor's decoded terms. A mechanical matching-receipt guard
cannot give an actor the other's vocabulary, original intended concepts, hidden
inventory or private production history. Different/missing receipts do not grant
joint execution or payment. The system does not fabricate a missing half-save.
Such a record can be cancelled or expire; automatic work-contract reaffirmation
is not implemented. Partnership reaffirmation is a separate verified feature.

One active work obligation is supported per person. Up to 16 personal histories
are retained. Negotiation has a 600-tick initiation cooldown and each speaker has
a 200-tick work-conversation budget. Resource reservation, advance payment and debt
are not created by speech. The price is fixed for this order; ordinary local
trade/delivery cannot replace it with a new quote while the obligation is active.

Actual AI execution

The worker's controller uses the saved obligation before ordinary prepared-goods
sales. It still yields to survival, owned food, safety and other higher-priority
physical constraints. A target must be independently visible within existing
search/home bounds. An order never reveals an absent customer's current location.
The matching durable receipt is a permission guard, not an input for learning the
other person's private beliefs or needs.

Hoe work uses existing ToolProduction and native wood recipe steps, retaining the
worker's own working hoe and food reserve. Bread work uses the existing physical
station approach, native wheat recipe and inventory commit. Inputs, capability
permissions, actual server recipes, reach, navigation admission and output capacity
remain authoritative. A successful recipe callback can create one production
credit in the worker's own record. A promise, old spare item, travel, missing
materials or a failed recipe does not create that credit.

Completion additionally requires an actual in-reach exchange of the specified
fresh wooden hoe or bread for the exact agreed emerald price. TradeMaterials checks
both actual inventories and capacity, preserves components and prevents stale
plan reuse. Each saved settlement records only its owner's before/after goods
and coins. The worker retains its own production evidence; the customer does not
receive the worker's private station coordinates or recipe memory. A broader
combined receipt exists only as a diagnostic session artifact for audits.

A completed paid handoff can increase trust as an actually kept promise. Replaying
a completed order does not charge again. Work outcomes never mint money, tools,
food, material refunds, replacement villagers or new physical capabilities.
Normal orderly restart is verified; atomic crash recovery across Minecraft entity
NBT and separate mind files is not claimed. A missing/inconsistent record or lost
physical output cannot be silently reconstructed into authority or free goods.

Interruption and unfulfilled obligations

Orders retain AGREED, WORKING, READY, PAUSED, COMPLETED, CANCELLED or EXPIRED
outcomes. Own unsafe conditions, missing materials, missing station/route, absent
peer, inconsistent terms, unavailable stock, payment or receiving capacity can
pause execution. Actual recovered conditions can resume permitted work; pausing
never erases a real production receipt or extends the deadline. Repeated waiting
on the same obstacle does not award work or inflate interruption counts every
think tick. Paused contract-generated preparation signals are withdrawn so a
legacy work path cannot continue an unconfirmed commission behind the guard.

Both actors independently expire their personal record on their own next think
at/after the deadline. Those updates need not occur on the same simulation tick;
current-consent checks already reject expired terms immediately. An unfinished
order records its last own obstacle and receives no completion/payment reward.
Failure or interruption is not automatically treated as intentional lying, and
no reputation penalty is inferred from an absent actor's private circumstances.

An explicit cancellation adapter withdraws the caller's own consent first and
communicates the full cancellation terms to a visible peer when budget permits.
The peer updates its own account only after decoding those words. Unheard or
misunderstood withdrawal cannot force continued joint execution. Cancellation
frames and consent invalidation have automated checks; a spontaneous autonomous
work-cancellation policy is not claimed by the native fixture.

Persistence and controls

Mind schema 15 adds personal work records. Schema-14 saves start with no invented
work, preserving partnership history, family history, words, identities and
existing learning. Children inherit neither jobs, debts nor their parents'
production memories. Unrelated participants and copied peer production evidence
are rejected. Unknown future schemas retain protection.

Frozen controls retain/read their exact saved terms and do not advance one-shot
economic obligations, production credits or payments. This follows the existing
local economy's frozen-mode exclusion, while language and policy checkpoints
remain fixed. Frozen performance of these one-shot jobs is not claimed; the
native control proves readability and absence of learned-state/payment mutation.

Verification and retained conditions

Automated tests cover precise frames, independent meanings, unknown grammar,
unsupported material/count/deadline, generic assent, confidently wrong prices,
matching/partial/mismatched saved receipts, exact own settlement deltas, no
completion from a promise or production alone, stale/replayed completion,
interruption/resumption, deadline, cancellation, bounded history, participant
privacy, schema migration, restart and exclusion of inherited obligations.
Existing recipe/trade tests continue checking real inputs, reserves and capacity.
