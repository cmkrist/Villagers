VERIFIABLE LANGUAGE AND GRAMMAR GOAL
2026-11-11

Objective

Give managed villagers compositional biome-based speech, listener-specific
interpretation, and language change through observable social encounters. Every
NPC interpretation must come from sounds and the listener's knowledge. No LLM
participates. Preserve saved words and origins, keep learning bounded, and verify
that interpretation influences existing AI interactions.

Acceptance criteria

1. Same-language speakers can express and parse complete subject/verb/object
   clauses. Four founding cultures have distinct deterministic word orders:
   plains SVO; desert/badlands SOV; forest/jungle/taiga VSO; snowy/frozen/peaks OVS.
2. Speaker intention and listener interpretation are separate. A listener may
   be 100% confident while choosing the wrong meaning or reversing semantic roles.
   Correctness is diagnostic data, not an AI decision oracle.
3. Missing required words, including negation/past markers, never disappear to
   produce a deceptively complete sentence. Developing children can still use
   their sparse primitive phrases, subject to existing complete-request checks.
4. Repeated varied observable encounters teach vocabulary and grammatical word
   order. Unobserved private meanings provide no learning evidence. Contradictory
   observable evidence can repair an incorrect word association.
5. Food requests, trade acceptance and family signals are checked through the
   listener's decoded sentence. Actual reach, inventory, willingness and other
   existing action requirements remain authoritative.
6. Learning survives save/reload and children inherit a small vocabulary and a
   weak preference for a parent's learned grammar. Older saves upgrade while
   keeping their existing words and cultural origin. Future schemas stay protected.
7. Generic gossip cannot unlock a named person's exact reputation or episode
   unless speech actually identifies that information. Current generic phrases
   therefore describe experiences but no longer disclose those hidden records.
8. An isolated Minecraft server demonstrates a wrong interpretation preventing
   a real handoff, repaired interpretation enabling a conserved real handoff,
   speech-driven grammar change, and original minds retaining grammar on restart.

Implementation

Clause represents subject, predicate, object, negation and past marking. Sentence
realizes these into learned sounds using the speaker's current word order.
The interpreter accepts only sounds, vocabulary and grammar; intended clauses
are retained separately for diagnostic comparison. Semicolon punctuation bounds
up to 8 clauses / 47 sounds. English translations are constructed from the listener's
interpretation, so they can also be confidently wrong.

Grammar stores six bounded order scores, up to 1000 each, decaying by halving on
saturation. Founders start with 6 observations for their biome order; children
start with 2 for a parent's selected order. Independently observed three-role
events add evidence only when known words identify one unambiguous order.
Contact can change the preferred spoken construction. Ties retain the existing
spoken convention; adoption requires strictly greater evidence. Passive vocabulary
acquisition retains native pronunciations while learning receptive synonyms.

LanguageLearning supplies a sound-only boundary for both NPCs and players.
Speech provides candidate meanings from public gestures, held objects, damage
and jointly visible blocks; speech intent itself supplies no private labels.
Existing request production no longer leaks the requester's private hunger as
player/NPC vocabulary evidence. Interpretation summaries enter the donor's
bounded memory when it hears a care request.

Mind schema 10 embeds grammar within Lexicon. Glossary schema 2 stores player
grammar and accepts schema 1 migration. Existing concept ordinals 0..27 remain
stable; syntax concepts are appended. Old adults acquire missing grammatical
words from their own founding language rather than a new private random dialect.
Sparse old children are not overwritten with adult vocabulary.

and requires the existing owner-accepted server EULA. It binds localhost:25618,
uses bounded first/restart processes, and records terminal reports and PID custody.

Limits and follow-on work

This achieves a bounded first language/grammar engine. Six authored word-order
patterns are learned; new grammatical categories do not emerge from scratch.
Confidence is an uncalibrated grammar-evidence heuristic, not a probability of truth.
Grammar preferences currently mix across encounters rather than keeping a separate
syntax model for each foreign language. Negation and past marking are supported
and tested by the engine; legacy activity adapters mostly emit present clauses.
Proper names, explicit third-party event references, novel compound words,
conversational clarification, knowledge testimony, promises, jobs, courtship
dialogue and general planning remain further milestones. Generic memories cannot
currently teach the full exact episode through speech; already-shared memories
remain available through the existing recall interface.

The runtime proof is server-side. No new visual-client acceptance or large
population compute benchmark is claimed. Primary and historical native worlds
were not advanced. This does not establish human-like understanding, consciousness,
autonomous grammar invention, sustained survival or population-level evolution.
