LANGUAGE AS PART OF VILLAGER LIFE — PRIORITY AND VERIFICATION
2026-11-11

Current priority

Develop clarification dialogue, personal beliefs, relationship conversations and
learned grammatical constructions through the existing language engine. No LLM
may speak or interpret on a villager's behalf. These take priority over cosmetic
dialogue expansion. Existing native survival work and society saves remain separate.

Implemented acceptance cases

1. Each mind retains up to 64 attributed beliefs. A belief contains a resolved
   conversational subject, predicate, object, optional participant reference,
   negation, tense, source, confidence, observation time and expiry. Generic nouns
   do not reveal a named third party. A listener stores its interpretation even
   when that interpretation is wrong.
2. Testimony remains separate from observation. Repetition by one speaker cannot
   promote hearsay into independent evidence or increase its confidence. An actual
   observation can confirm/contradict testimony while preserving attribution.
   An updated statement can supersede its source's older statement. No record is
   copied from another villager's private memory or inventory.
3. Fresh, positively interpreted HAVE FOOD testimony influences ordering among
   actually visible, otherwise eligible food-help candidates. It grants no items,
   remote stock access, expanded reach or navigation permission. Leads expire.
4. A confused listener can repeat its own interpretation or ask again. The
   speaker decodes that echo and compares it with its own intention. A decoded
   denial retracts the listener's tentative interpretation, without blaming a
   source for deliberate deception. Confirmation alone supplies no objective
   truth: symmetric mistranslations can remain undetected.
5. A food-word repair requires a real food item publicly held by a participant
   and joint line of sight. Its sound is demonstrated against observable food.
   Evidence accumulates across encounters; a request's private intention never
   supplies a per-word translation. Repair/relationship attention is bounded to
   one opportunity per participant per 200 ticks. Ordinary request and speech
   budgets still apply.
6. Ordinary companion visits require an interpreted invitation, the receiver's
   independent willingness, and an interpreted promise. Accepted short agreements
   influence AI: the host waits for company rather than starting another elective
   walk. Survival, food, health and existing higher-priority work still take
   precedence. Actual sustained contact fulfills the promise and provides trust
   and social relief; words alone do not award these benefits.
7. Grammar can induce bounded surface constructions from known sounds aligned
   with independently observed events. It can learn a two-word state construction
   with an implicit copula or a repeated-role pattern beyond the six founding
   orders. Repeated evidence permits interpretation; evidence exceeding the
   existing production preference permits adoption. Patterns generalize to new
   combinations of known words. Ambiguous constructions trigger uncertainty.
8. Ordinary conversations can occasionally propose a shorter state construction
   for actual visible walking or injury. The speaker's intention does not teach
   listeners the missing grammar. Children inherit weak exposure to established
   constructions, not instant mastery. At most eight patterns are stored; evidence
   is bounded and decays together on saturation. Sentences allow at most eight
   clauses and 63 sounds.
9. Mind schema 11 persists beliefs and learned constructions, with migration from
   schema 10 retaining old vocabulary and origin. Player glossary schema 3
   preserves earlier evidence while adding construction state. Future schemas
   remain protected. Frozen research controls can use already-understood agreements
   while their learned language, beliefs and opinions remain fixed. The language command exposes belief, construction, repair
   and agreement counts for inspection.

Verification

Automated checks cover source attribution, fallible interpretation, expiry,
contradiction, retraction, source withdrawal, finite state, understood promises,
confirmation and symmetric errors, construction induction/generalization,
unknown markers, ambiguity, inheritance and restart/migration.

The native fixture tools/language-life-server-test.py creates a NEW peaceful
world. Requester/donor and visitor/host occupy separate chambers. Declared setup:
requester starts at 4 food; a generous donor starts at 20 food/20 saturation with
one inventory bread and one actual publicly held bread; the lonely visitor has
three declared prior food-favour impressions of the host; visitor/host each own
six bread. There are no post-setup position, food, health, pose or grammar setters.

The fixture schedules requests while retaining real request/repair cooldowns.
It requires denied mistranslation, automatic grounded word repair, one conserved
real bread transfer, an autonomously completed understood social visit, adoption
of a learned construction, generalization to a new phrase, and exact restart
preservation for all four original minds. Controlled compression opportunities
respect speech timing and require actual walking or a real visible injury.
At most twelve native one-point damage events, only at full health, can supply
repeated injury observations; regeneration and food consumption remain native.
These are declared experimental stimuli, not proof of spontaneous emergence.

Verified: 606 Java tests and 19 Python tests passed. Native fixture
language-life-v7 passed at 2537 simulation ticks: 4 grounded repairs,
4 spoken denials, one conserved real bread transfer, an autonomous social
visit, and a generalized compact construction after 7 observed opportunities.
All four original bodies survived. Adaptive restart preserved their language and
beliefs. A subsequent frozen-control run understood an agreement while preserving
learned vocabulary, constructions, beliefs and opinions.

Final results, test counts, artifact hashes and fixture conditions are stored in
docs/evidence/2026-11-11/language-life/verification.json.
Previous fixtures remain under runs/language-life-v1 through subsequent versions.
Their stopped/failed outcomes are retained: symmetric errors surviving an echo,
food-provisioning preventing social activity, and sparse public movement samples
were treated as findings, not silently relabeled as successful tests. The third
phase uses a checkpoint of the actual adaptive restart state, rather than
incorrectly comparing that later state with the original earlier checkpoint.

source tools/java-env.sh
villagers_select_java
./tools/test.sh
./gradlew -I tools/probe-classpath.gradle writeProbeClasspath
python3 tools/language-life-server-test.py --runtime-name language-life-FRESH

Replace FRESH with a new lowercase name. The runner refuses existing runtimes,
requires the prepared server's owner-accepted EULA, binds localhost:25619 and
records owned process custody, pinned artifacts, terminal results, restart and a
frozen-control compatibility check.

Priority following this bounded milestone

Extend explicit referents and grounded testimony to places and named third
parties; add multi-turn questions and practical promises; develop persistent
partnership/courtship conversations and work agreements; distinguish receptive
grammar by foreign language; expand word formation and teaching across childhood.
Evaluate how learned conventions spread across generations and contact networks.

Limits

Beliefs are bounded propositions, not a general causal model or arbitrary nested
reasoning. Short rendezvous execution state is transient; the attributed promise
and witnessed outcome persist. This does not yet implement marriage dialogue,
employment contracts, unrestricted free-text conversation, or general planning.
Construction induction operates over authored semantic roles and a constrained
copula-omission innovation; it does not invent new semantic categories or prove
unrestricted grammar emergence. Confidence is a heuristic, not a calibrated
probability. The native proof is a declared provisioned server experiment; it
does not prove visual-client UX, natural-village survival or population scaling.
